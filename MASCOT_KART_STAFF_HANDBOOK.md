# Sweetie's Apple iStadium Mascot Kart Race Staff Handbook

## Purpose
This handbook turns the official race rulebook into a practical race-day guide for staff, marshals, hosts, mechanics, safety leads, and guest support teams.

## Staff Roles
| Role | Main Responsibility | Must Confirm Before Opening |
|---|---|---|
| Race Director | Final authority for starts, stops, penalties, and restarts | Race calendar, staffing roster, radio channel |
| Safety Lead | Track safety, incident response, and restart clearance | Medical station, fire kits, evacuation routes |
| Pit Marshal | Pit lane lineup, kart release, and pit speed control | Wristbands, kart IDs, pit lane barriers |
| Track Marshals | Corner monitoring, signals, and driver support | Signal lights, radios, safe standing zones |
| Kart Technician | Kart inspection, battery checks, repairs, and charging | Brakes, steering, bump rails, governors |
| Guest Support | Family check-in, youth pickup, accessibility support | Guardian list, quiet room, spectator access |
| Scorekeeper | Timing, points, penalties, and scoreboard updates | Timing system, class roster, awards sheet |

## Opening Checklist
1. Walk the full track and pit lane.
2. Confirm anti-slip surface checks are complete.
3. Inspect barriers, gates, marshal stations, and spectator zones.
4. Test race lights, timing screens, radios, and public announcements.
5. Inspect each kart for brakes, steering, bump rails, lights, battery health, and speed governor settings.
6. Confirm medical response coverage and emergency communication.
7. Review the day's race schedule with all staff.

## Session Flow
1. Guest support checks in drivers and guardians.
2. Pit marshal verifies wristbands and class eligibility.
3. Safety lead or host delivers the safety briefing.
4. Kart technician releases inspected karts to pit lane.
5. Race director starts warm-up lap.
6. Track marshals observe for unsafe driving or equipment issues.
7. Race director starts the timed session.
8. Scorekeeper records results, penalties, and bonuses.
9. Pit marshal guides drivers back to controlled unload.
10. Guest support handles youth release checkpoints.

## Standard Announcements
- **Before briefing:** "Drivers and families, please gather at the Apple iStadium briefing zone."
- **Before warm-up:** "This is a controlled warm-up lap. No passing and no contact."
- **Yellow signal:** "Caution is active. Slow down and do not pass."
- **Red signal:** "Stop safely in the nearest safe zone and wait for staff instructions."
- **Awards:** "Thank you for racing with teamwork, safety, and Sweetie spirit."

## Incident Response Plan
1. Race director triggers yellow or red signal.
2. Track marshals secure the affected area.
3. Safety lead decides whether medical or technical support is needed.
4. Kart technician removes or locks out unsafe equipment.
5. Scorekeeper records the incident and any penalty.
6. Race director and safety lead approve restart only after the track is clear.

## Closing Checklist
1. Save timing and scoring records.
2. Complete incident logs and maintenance notes.
3. Sanitize helmets, collars, gloves, and shared gear.
4. Charge karts according to the battery rotation plan.
5. Reset barriers, signs, wristband supplies, and briefing area.
6. Hold a five-minute staff debrief for lessons learned.
