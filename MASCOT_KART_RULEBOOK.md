# Sweetie's Apple iStadium Mascot Kart Race Rulebook

## 1) Official Race Identity
- **Event Name:** Sweetie Mascot Kart Grand Prix
- **Venue:** Apple iStadium (Right Eye Window District) with optional Apple iSpeedway exhibition loop
- **Core Motto:** Safe fun for kids, families, mascots, junior mascots, and partners
- **Revision:** 1.2

## 2) Race Classes and Session Limits
| Class | Driver Group | Session Length | Max Karts | Top Speed Cap |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Class K (Kids Fun Laps) | Ages 6-12 | 8 minutes | 8 | 8 mph |
| Class J (Junior Mascot Cup) | Ages 10-17 | 10 minutes | 10 | 12 mph |
| Class A (Adult Mascot Challenge) | Ages 18+ | 12 minutes | 12 | 18 mph |
| Class P (Partner Team Relay) | Approved mixed teams | 16 minutes | 12 | 16 mph |
| Class X (All-Star Exhibition) | Special invitational | variable | 12 | director set |

## 3) Track Format
- Indoor primary track inside Apple iStadium
- Optional linked tunnel section for exhibition events
- Clearly marked start/finish line, pit lane, safety zones, and marshal stations
- LED lane lights for visibility and race control
- Anti-slip surface checks required before each session block

## 4) Kart Safety Requirements
- Padded chassis and bump rails
- Remote speed governor enabled at all times
- Auto-brake when obstacle or collision risk is detected
- Front and rear safety lighting
- Vehicle ID number visible on each kart
- Braking, steering, and battery health pass pre-race inspection

## 5) Driver Safety Equipment
- Properly fitted helmet
- Neck support collar for junior classes
- Gloves and closed-toe shoes
- Optional elbow/knee padding for youth sessions
- Mascot headgear must be ventilation-approved for racing use

## 6) Age and Eligibility
- **Kids Fun Laps:** Age 6-12 with guardian check-in
- **Junior Mascot Cup:** Age 10-17 with safety briefing completion
- **Adult Mascot Challenge:** Age 18+
- **Partner Team Relay:** Mixed ages allowed only in approved team categories
- Drivers may be moved to a lower-speed class by safety staff discretion

## 7) Pre-Race Procedures (Required)
1. Registration and wristband assignment
2. Safety inspection of kart and equipment
3. Driver briefing and flag/light signal review
4. Warm-up lap at controlled speed
5. Final lineup and start sequence

A session cannot start unless all five checks are complete.

## 8) Start and Race Control Signals
- Rolling start for kids and junior classes
- Grid start permitted for adult and partner classes
- Race director controls all starts, pauses, and restarts
- **Green:** race active
- **Yellow:** caution/no passing
- **Red:** immediate stop in safe zone
- **Blue:** faster kart approaching; maintain line
- **Black:** report to pit for conduct review

## 9) Racing Conduct Rules
- No intentional contact or blocking
- No dangerous swerving or reverse driving on active track
- Respect marshal instructions immediately
- Pit lane speed limit always enforced
- Sportsmanship required before, during, and after races

## 10) Penalty Ladder
| Violation Level | Examples | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | first minor line violation | warning |
| Level 2 | repeat minor or unsafe merge | +10 seconds |
| Level 3 | avoidable contact or ignoring yellow | stop-and-go |
| Level 4 | deliberate dangerous behavior | disqualification |
| Level 5 | severe/repeated misconduct | event suspension |

## 11) Scoring, Points, and Awards
### Points by Finish
- 1st: 10
- 2nd: 8
- 3rd: 6
- 4th: 5
- 5th: 4
- 6th: 3
- 7th: 2
- 8th+: 1

### Bonus
- Clean-race bonus: +1
- Team spirit bonus (relay): +1

### Awards
- Separate podiums by class
- Family and team spirit awards
- Seasonal championship for recurring events

## 12) Emergency Protocol
- On-track marshals at designated stations
- Medical response team on standby
- Fire safety kits and communication panels at all corners
- Evacuation routes announced during briefing
- Incident documentation required after any significant event
- Restart only after race director + safety lead clearance

## 13) Spectator and Family Access
- Protected viewing zones with barrier systems
- Accessible seating and stroller-friendly routes
- Live timing and leaderboard screens
- Quiet room and family support services nearby
- Youth pickup checkpoint required for Class K and Class J release

## 14) Media and Broadcast
- Consent-based photo/video policy
- Highlight reels for class finals
- Replay review for incident verification
- Scoreboard announcements after each heat
- No close-range flash photography in active race zones

## 15) Operations Checklist
- Open: track walk, marshal assignment, equipment checks, medical confirmation
- Live: lap timing active, radios tested, pit lane supervised, crowd barriers monitored
- Close: incident log review, battery charging plan, sanitation, next-day prep

## 16) Planning Roadmap (Make More Plans)
### 16.1 Daily Plan
- **Morning (open prep):** surface checks, kart readiness, staffing confirmation
- **Midday (peak family sessions):** prioritize Class K and Class J rotations
- **Afternoon (league heats):** run Class A and Class P qualifiers
- **Evening (showcase):** host Class X exhibition and awards ceremony

### 16.2 Weekly Plan
- **Monday:** safety drills + marshal refresher
- **Tuesday:** school and junior mascot league sessions
- **Wednesday:** family fun day with reduced speed classes
- **Thursday:** partner relay training blocks
- **Friday:** open qualifying events
- **Saturday:** championship rounds + finals
- **Sunday:** community showcase + maintenance reset

### 16.3 Monthly Plan
- Week 1: youth development challenge
- Week 2: mascot sportsmanship cup
- Week 3: partner relay invitational
- Week 4: Sweetie Grand Championship

### 16.4 Seasonal Plan
- **Spring Season:** beginner and safety-first development
- **Summer Season:** high-volume family festival racing
- **Fall Season:** school league and partner cup focus
- **Winter Season:** indoor championship and all-star exhibitions

### 16.5 Growth Plan
- Add second training loop if average wait times exceed 25 minutes
- Expand marshal teams when daily attendance passes target thresholds
- Add additional accessible viewing sections based on family demand
- Review points system each season with safety committee approval

## 17) Official Closing Statement
Apple iStadium Mascot Kart Race combines joy, fairness, safety, and friendly competition. Every race must protect children, support families, and celebrate teamwork.
