# Sweetie's Mascot Kart Quick Rules for Kids

## Remember Sweetie's 5 Race Promises
1. **I will listen to the marshals.**
2. **I will keep both hands on the wheel.**
3. **I will drive safely and stay in my lane.**
4. **I will slow down for yellow and stop for red.**
5. **I will cheer for everyone and race with kindness.**

## Light Signals
| Light | What It Means | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Go racing | Drive safely |
| Yellow | Caution | Slow down and do not pass |
| Red | Stop | Stop in a safe place |
| Blue | Faster kart nearby | Keep your line |
| Black | Pit check | Go to the pit marshal |

## Before You Race
- Put on your helmet and safety gear.
- Listen to the safety briefing.
- Ask a marshal if you are confused.
- Take the warm-up lap slowly.

## During The Race
- No bumping on purpose.
- No driving backward.
- No cutting across the track.
- Give space to other drivers.
- Have fun and be brave like the mascots.

## After The Race
- Drive slowly into the pit lane.
- Wait for a marshal before getting out.
- Meet your guardian at the pickup checkpoint.
- Clap for every racer.
